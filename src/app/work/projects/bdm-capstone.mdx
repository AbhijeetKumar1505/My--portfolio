## Overview

**Optimizing User Engagement and Platform Visibility for Ments** — Business Data Management (BDM) Capstone Project.

A survey-based data analysis of the early-stage collaboration platform **Ments**, examining user engagement, retention, churn, and discoverability, with data-backed recommendations to improve growth.

Analysis covers responses from **~1,600 respondents** collected over ~3 months.

## Business questions

1. **Engagement & retention** — How often do users actually use the platform?
2. **Churn** — Who is leaving, and why?
3. **Discoverability (SEO)** — How do users find Ments, and how visible is it organically?

## Headline findings

- Average satisfaction **~2.94 / 5** and simplicity **~2.99 / 5** — both below neutral
- **~65–70%** of users engage only occasionally or rarely; few form a daily habit
- Churn is driven by platform shortcomings (technical issues, low activity, lack of value), not random factors
- Weak organic visibility — most users arrive via direct/branded search rather than problem-based search or social
- Churned users report lower satisfaction than active users, confirming a satisfaction–churn link

## Analysis workflow

1. Load & clean the survey XLSX; rename columns to working fields
2. Core metrics — average satisfaction and simplicity
3. Distributions — usage frequency, satisfaction, simplicity
4. Churn analysis — rate and reasons for stopping
5. Discoverability — acquisition channels and Google findability
6. Attention & competition — overwhelm and active-platform counts
7. Segmentation — satisfaction by user type (student, engineer, freelancer, founder, PM, investor)
8. Correlation — satisfaction vs. simplicity
9. Consolidated insights and recommendations

## Recommendations

- **Onboarding & UX** — guided onboarding and a simpler interface to lift satisfaction/simplicity scores
- **Engagement** — notifications, weekly prompts, and personalized content to drive return visits
- **SEO** — target collaboration/problem keywords and improve metadata for organic reach
- **Technical & retention** — fix bugs and load times; add retention/loyalty programs to reduce churn
